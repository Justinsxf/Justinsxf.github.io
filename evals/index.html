What's tested

The AI turns a syllabus into exams, quizzes, homework, papers and speeches on the calendar. It happens in two places, each with its own prompt:

  • Onboarding: you drop in several files at sign-up. Each is read on its own, and the AI also works out the course and the term dates. It's a new user's first impression, so it matters most.
  • Course page: adding a syllabus to a course you already have. Only dated items are pulled.

Errors, worst first: invented date > unclear date shown as certain > missed item > extra item (fine if the date really is in the syllabus).

Every answer cites the line it came from (see Answer key).

How it's scored

  • Written date: same date and a matching type (homework, papers and projects count as one type).
  • Unclear date: leave it empty, or pick a candidate and flag it "to confirm". A firm date counts as unflagged.
  • Weekly homework: may be expanded week by week, but every expanded item must be flagged.
  • No date: list it and leave the date empty.
  • Invented: a date that isn't in the syllabus, or a year outside the term.
  • Time: if the syllabus gives a time, it must match. If it only says "in class", an empty time is fine; a different time is wrong.
  • Pass: every item found, 0 invented, 0 unflagged, 0 wrong times.
